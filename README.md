# Privacy Policy for Vertex CAD

**Effective Date:** October 29, 2026

Vertex CAD is developed by Waldner Studio ("we", "us"). This policy explains how information is handled in the Vertex CAD Android app.

---

### 1. Information Collection
* **No personal data:** We do not collect, transmit, store or share any personal information, user accounts or device identifiers. There is no sign-in.
* **No tracking or analytics:** The app contains no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or other tracking SDKs.
* **No servers of ours:** Vertex CAD does not send your files or any other data to us or to any cloud service.

---

### 2. Files and Local Storage
* **Local processing:** CAD files (STEP, IGES, DXF, Inventor, PDF and ZIP packages) are opened and rendered on your device.
* **Temporary cache:** Opening a file from another app creates a temporary copy in the app's cache, which is cleaned up automatically.
* **On-device settings:** App settings (theme, display options) and PC Inventor Bridge pairing details (the PC address you enter and this device's pairing key) are saved only on your device. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes them.
* **Printing and sharing:** Exported images and print snapshots are handled by Android's own share and print services, and go only where you choose to send them.

---

### 3. Optional PC Inventor Bridge
Vertex CAD can optionally connect to the free PC Inventor Bridge, which you install on your own Windows PC that has Autodesk Inventor (download: https://inventor-pc-bridge.github.io/pc-bridge/).
* **Your own PC only:** When you choose to convert an Inventor part (.ipt), assembly (.iam) or assembly package, the file is sent directly from your phone to the bridge on your PC, over your own private network (your home network or a VPN you run). The converted model is sent back to your phone.
* **Nothing goes through us:** The connection is between your devices only. Neither we nor any third party receive your files.
* **Pairing:** Pairing uses a code shown in the bridge window on your PC. The pairing details are stored on your phone and on your PC only.
* **Your choice:** Nothing is sent to the bridge unless you pair with it and choose to convert a file.

---

### 4. Network Use
* **PDF viewer component:** When you open a PDF, the app may download the open-source PDF.js rendering component from the cdnjs.cloudflare.com content network. Only that component is requested; your PDF file stays on your device. Cloudflare may log standard request details such as IP address, as described in the [Cloudflare Privacy Policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/).
* **Bridge download link:** Opening or sharing the bridge download link uses your browser or Android's share sheet.

---

### 5. Device Permissions
* **`INTERNET`:** Used for the optional connection to the PC Inventor Bridge on your own network and for loading the PDF viewer component.
* **`READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE` / `READ_MEDIA_IMAGES`:** Used only to open files you select and to save exported images.

---

### 6. Children
Vertex CAD is a technical tool for general audiences and is not directed at children. It does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

---

### 7. Changes to This Policy
If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new effective date.
